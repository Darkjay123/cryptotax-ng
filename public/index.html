How it works

Paste your wallet

Add your Tron, EVM or Bitcoin addresses, or drop the statements from your exchange or off-ramp app. Up to five wallets and six files.

We do the reading

Every transfer, swap, sale and reward is read, valued in dollars at the day's market price, and turned into naira at the official CBN rate for that day.

See it, file it

You get the tax estimate in naira, a line-by-line story you can check, and a filing-ready PDF. Anything unclear is asked, never guessed.
